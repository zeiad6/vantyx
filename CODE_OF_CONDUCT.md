# Code of conduct

Vantyx is a technical project. Participation requires professional, patient, and
specific communication.

## Expected behavior

- Critique code, designs, and evidence rather than people.
- Assume good faith, but ask for clarification when a claim is unclear.
- Keep discussions on topic and reproducible.
- Respect privacy: do not publish another person's private information.
- Accept that maintainers may reject a change to protect scope or correctness.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, or sexualized attention.
- Personal attacks, sustained disruption, or deliberate misinformation.
- Publishing private communications or credentials without permission.
- Submitting code or benchmarks that the contributor knows are fabricated.

## Enforcement

Report concerns privately through the repository's **Security** tab so the
maintainers can review them without public exposure. Maintainers may edit or
remove comments, close threads, or block accounts. Reports are handled on a
best-effort basis and kept confidential as far as practical.

This policy applies in repository spaces and in public spaces when someone is
representing the project.
