## Limits

- The refusal is lexical. It does not resolve symbolic links, normalize
  intermediate `.`/`..` components, or prevent `link/sub` from traversing a
  link. Check-then-act races remain.
- There is no confirmation, trash, dry run, rollback, depth limit,
  entry-count limit, atomic staging, `Result` recovery, or sandbox.
- The classifier recognizes `/` separators. Windows interpreter paths that use
  `\` are not classified, and Windows native support remains unverified.
- Deep trees still consume the native C call stack.
- Native allocations remain intentionally leaked in v0.1.
