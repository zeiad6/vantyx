## Defects and fixes

1. `Emitter::condition` treated `({ ... })` as already parenthesized. Any call
   or effectful expression in an `if`/`elif`/`while` condition produced
   `if ({ ... })`, which clang rejects. The helper now wraps only rendered
   conditions that start with `({`, so comparisons keep their existing
   parenthesization and do not trigger `-Wparentheses-equality`.
2. `Emitter::binary_expr` hoisted both operands of `and`/`or` into
   temporaries whenever both had effects. That evaluated the right operand
   even when the left operand decided the result. The backend now lowers an
   effectful right operand through a lazy conditional expression in a `bool`
   statement expression; effect-free right operands use ordinary `&&`/`||`.
3. Added `tools/differential_fuzz.py`, a deterministic standard-library-only
   generator for three typed program shapes. It found both defects. CI runs 50
   iterations on seed 1.

## Decisions

- Fix the condition wrapper in `condition`, not at every emission site, to
  avoid clang warnings on comparisons.
- Preserve lazy evaluation for effectful `and`/`or` operands; keep `&&`/`||`
  when the right operand is effect-free.
- Keep the fuzzer dependency-free and deterministic; wire a fixed seed into
  CI; save failing programs for regression tests.
- Record the contract in ADR-0036; do not change the `E0401` boundary.
