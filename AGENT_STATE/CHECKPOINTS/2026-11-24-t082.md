## Defect

Native `Unit ==`/`!=` lowered to a constant and emitted neither operand.
`src/codegen_c.rs::binary_expr` hoisted operands only when both sides had
observable effects, so a comparison with one effectful side dropped that side.
The interpreter evaluates both operands left to right.

Observed pre-fix divergence on `/tmp/opencode/p32/unit.vtx`:

- interpreter: `decl`, `()`, `cmp`, `true`, `cmp2`, `false`
- native: `decl`, `()`, `true`, `false`

## Fix

`binary_expr` now hoists both operands into typed temporaries whenever either
operand has effects. Non-`and`/`or` binaries always evaluate both sides, and the
hoist also makes source order compiler-independent. The deliberate
short-circuit exception for `and`/`or` (ADR-0036) is unchanged.

## Tests

- e2e `unit_equality_evaluates_both_operands` pins the full stdout sequence,
  including single-effect and two-effect forms for `==` and `!=`.
- native `unit_equality_evaluates_both_operands` compares both engines.
- `tools/differential_fuzz.py::functions_program` compares `unit`/`note(...)`
  equality in single-effect and two-effect forms.

## Specification

- §5.3 states that both operands are evaluated left to right even when the
  static type makes the result constant.
- §11 documents the native hoist and the `Unit` equality case.
